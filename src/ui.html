03 — KNOW YOUR DATA

Precision starts with
honest limits.

01 / INPUTS

Reference science data

Ten candidate coordinates, supplied hourly JPL Horizons DE441 Sun/Earth directions and NASA LOLA-derived local skyline values. This viewer reuses prepared reference samples; it does not independently regenerate those ephemerides or terrain horizons.

02 / VISIBILITY

Terrain-aware checks

A sample passes only when both bodies stand above their respective azimuth-specific terrain horizons plus the chosen angular safety margin. Margins are applied to both Sun and Earth.

03 / UNCERTAINTY

Model, not mission approval

Continuous windows are estimated by straight-line interpolation between one-hour samples. Terrain slope summaries cannot identify local rocks, link budget, illumination storage, or provide certified landing-safety assessments.

04 — PROVENANCE & TRANSPARENCY

Evidence before certainty.

This educational viewer analyzes participant-supplied reference samples associated with JPL Horizons DE441 Sun/Earth geometry and NASA LRO LOLA-derived terrain skylines. The raw ephemerides, elevation grids and original preprocessing were not independently regenerated in this application; the sampled inputs are not live NASA feeds.

AI / SI ASSISTANCE

Generative AI was used to help implement UI and analysis code, automated tests, documentation, and presentation materials. The sample data came from supplied research references; no independent validation of that data is claimed. This is not a NASA-certified or operational landing tool.
